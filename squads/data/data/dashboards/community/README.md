# Community Dashboards

This subdirectory contains specifications and documentation for community health and engagement dashboards.

## Purpose

Community dashboards track the vitality of the community ecosystem including member growth, content production, peer support effectiveness, and loop health. This directory holds the specifications for monitoring community as a strategic product extension.

## What Goes Here

- Dashboard specifications for community-related dashboards
- Community loop health visualization documentation
- Member growth and activity trend dashboard specs
- Content production and consumption ratio dashboard configurations
- Peer support effectiveness tracking dashboard documentation
- Change logs and community team feedback

## Conventions

- Name files after the dashboard: `community-overview-spec.md`
- Note data quality limitations prominently on community dashboards (see data-quality-registry.yaml)
- Track both leading indicators (activity) and lagging indicators (member retention)
- Include loop health metrics from the community registry
- Support filtering by member type, activity level, and content category
- Review with the community lead monthly to ensure alignment with community strategy
