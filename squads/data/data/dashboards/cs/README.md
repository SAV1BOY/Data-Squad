# Customer Success Dashboards

This subdirectory contains specifications and documentation for customer success team dashboards.

## Purpose

CS dashboards empower the customer success team with health scores, ticket trends, and account-level insights needed to prioritize outreach and interventions. This directory ensures these operational dashboards are well-specified and maintained.

## What Goes Here

- Dashboard specifications for CS-facing dashboards
- Health score overview and drill-down dashboard documentation
- Ticket volume and resolution tracking dashboard specs
- Account-level detail dashboard configurations
- CS team workload and capacity dashboard documentation
- Change logs and CS team feedback

## Conventions

- Name files after the dashboard: `cs-health-overview-spec.md`
- CS dashboards should prominently display account health status with drill-down capability
- Include both aggregate views and individual account detail views
- Ensure health score version is clearly labeled on all dashboards (reference cs-health-registry.yaml)
- Support filtering by health status, account segment, and CS owner
- Review with CS leadership monthly to ensure dashboards support their workflow
