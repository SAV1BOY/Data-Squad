# Revenue Metrics

This subdirectory contains analyses and documentation for revenue-related metrics including ARPU, MRR, expansion, and contraction.

## Purpose

Revenue metrics quantify the financial health of the business. This directory tracks revenue trends, unit economics, and the levers that drive revenue growth or contraction.

## What Goes Here

- MRR and ARR trend reports with decomposition (new, expansion, contraction, churn)
- ARPU analyses by segment, cohort, and plan tier
- Expansion revenue tracking and upsell effectiveness
- Revenue concentration analyses (dependency on top accounts)
- Pricing impact analyses and elasticity studies
- Revenue forecasts and scenario modeling

## Conventions

- Name files with the metric and period: `mrr-decomposition-feb-2026.md`
- Always decompose MRR changes into new, expansion, contraction, and churned components
- Track revenue per segment to identify concentration risk
- Note any billing system quirks that affect revenue data (see data-quality-registry.yaml for known issues)
- Align revenue reporting periods with the finance team's accounting calendar
