# Occam's Razor Blog (Avinash Kaushik): Canonical Articles Index

Index of the most influential articles from Avinash Kaushik's Occam's Razor blog on digital analytics and data-driven marketing.

---

## Foundational Frameworks

- **"Web Analytics 2.0: The Art of Customer Centricity"** - Kaushik's core framework: move beyond clickstream data to combine quantitative (what), qualitative (why), competitive (what else), and outcome (so what) data. The foundation for modern digital analytics practice.
- **"The 10/90 Rule for Analytics Success"** - Iconic article arguing that companies should spend 10% of their analytics budget on tools and 90% on people who can analyze data and drive action. Still one of the most cited analytics strategy articles.
- **"See-Think-Do-Care Framework"** - Marketing framework replacing the traditional funnel with intent-based stages: See (broadest audience), Think (those with some commercial intent), Do (those ready to act), Care (existing customers). Each stage requires different metrics.

## Metrics and KPIs

- **"Best Metrics for Digital Marketing"** - Comprehensive guide to choosing the right metrics at each stage of the customer journey. Covers: acquisition metrics (click-through rate, CPC, impression share), behavior metrics (bounce rate, pages per session, time on site), and outcome metrics (conversion rate, revenue per visitor, per-visit goal value).
- **"The Difference Between Macro and Micro Conversions"** - Why tracking only macro conversions (purchases, sign-ups) misses 95% of the optimization story. How to identify and measure micro conversions (email sign-up, video view, tool usage) that predict macro outcomes.
- **"Six Metrics Every Executive Should Care About"** - Kaushik's recommended exec dashboard: conversion rate, average order value, days to transaction, visits-to-purchase, cost per acquisition, and customer lifetime value. Explains why each matters and how to use them.
- **"Economic Value: Beyond Revenue"** - Framework for measuring total digital value, including non-revenue outcomes: lead generation, support deflection, brand awareness, and audience building. How to assign economic value to each.

## Analysis Techniques

- **"The Lean Analytics Cycle"** - Process for turning data into action: Question (what are we trying to learn?), Explore (what does the data say?), Explain (what does it mean?), Test (what should we try?), Implement (what are we changing?).
- **"Competitive Intelligence Analysis"** - How to use publicly available tools and data to benchmark against competitors. Covers market share estimation, content strategy analysis, and audience overlap measurement.
- **"Multi-Channel Attribution Analysis"** - Kaushik's approach to attribution: start with last-click (imperfect but actionable), move to weighted attribution, and eventually to incrementality testing. Practical advice for companies at different maturity levels.
- **"Advanced Segmentation Techniques"** - Beyond demographics: behavioral segmentation (what they do), outcome-based segmentation (what they achieve), and acquisition-based segmentation (how they arrived). How to layer segments for deeper insight.

## Data Storytelling and Communication

- **"Data Storytelling: How to Present Analytics"** - Guide to presenting data to non-technical audiences: lead with the action, provide context, use comparison (vs. benchmark, vs. last period, vs. goal), and always include "so what?"
- **"Choosing the Right Data Visualization"** - When to use tables, line charts, bar charts, scatter plots, and heatmaps. Specific guidance on avoiding misleading visualizations and designing for clarity.
- **"How to Write Actionable Analytics Reports"** - Report structure: finding, context, implication, recommendation. Every page must include at least one recommendation. "If there's no action, there's no analysis."

## Testing and Experimentation

- **"A/B Testing for Beginners"** - Foundational guide covering hypothesis formation, sample size calculation, test duration, and statistical significance interpretation. Explains common mistakes and how to avoid them.
- **"The Ultimate Guide to Testing Strategies"** - Beyond A/B: multivariate testing, multi-armed bandits, and incrementality testing. When to use each approach and how to set up a testing program.

## Organizational Analytics

- **"Building an Analytics-Driven Organization"** - Maturity model for analytics adoption: Level 1 (reporting), Level 2 (analysis), Level 3 (optimization), Level 4 (prediction), Level 5 (transformation). Assessment criteria and advancement strategies for each level.
- **"The Analytics Career Guide"** - Roles, skills, and career paths in analytics. Covers: reporting analysts, business analysts, data scientists, analytics engineers, and analytics leaders. What each role does and how they interact.
- **"Hiring Great Analysts"** - What to look for beyond technical skills: intellectual curiosity, business acumen, communication ability, and bias for action. Interview questions and assessment rubrics.
