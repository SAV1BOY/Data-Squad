# Growth Lever Phrases

## Purpose

Standardized phrases for communicating about growth levers: acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, and referral. These phrases connect data findings to specific growth mechanisms.

## Acquisition Lever

- "New user acquisition this [period] was [X], [up/down] [Y]% from [prior period]."
- "The top acquisition channel is [channel], contributing [X]% of new users at a CAC of $[Y]."
- "Paid acquisition efficiency [improved/declined]: CAC moved from $[X] to $[Y], a [Z]% change."
- "Organic acquisition accounted for [X]% of new users, driven primarily by [source]."
- "The acquisition funnel shows the biggest drop-off at [stage], where [X]% of visitors do not proceed."
- "Channel mix has shifted: [channel A] grew from [X]% to [Y]% of total acquisition, while [channel B] declined."

## Activation Lever

- "Activation rate (defined as [definition]) is [X]%, [up/down] [Y] points from [prior period]."
- "Users who complete [specific action] within [time window] retain at [X]x the rate of those who do not."
- "The activation bottleneck is at [step]: [X]% of users reach this point but only [Y]% complete it."
- "Time to activation averaged [X] [minutes/hours/days] this [period], [faster/slower] than the [Z] target."
- "Activation by segment: [segment A] activates at [X]%, [segment B] at [Y]%. The gap suggests [interpretation]."
- "The most predictive activation action is [action]. Users who perform it within [time] are [X]x more likely to become long-term active users."

## Retention Lever

- "Retention at [time period] is [X]%, [direction] [Y] points versus [comparison period]."
- "The retention curve flattens at [week/month X], indicating that users who reach this point tend to stay."
- "Cohort analysis shows [improving/declining/stable] retention: the [recent cohort] is performing [better/worse/similarly] to [older cohort] at the same maturity."
- "The biggest retention risk is in [segment], where [X]-day retention is [Y]% below the overall average."
- "Users who [specific behavior pattern] have [X]% higher retention than those who do not."
- "Reactivation of dormant users contributed [X] returning users this [period], representing [Y]% of total active users."

## Revenue Lever

- "MRR grew to $[X], an increase of [Y]% driven by [primary source: new / expansion / pricing]."
- "Expansion revenue accounted for [X]% of total revenue growth this [period], outpacing new business."
- "ARPU [increased/decreased] from $[X] to $[Y], driven by [mix shift / pricing change / feature adoption]."
- "Revenue at risk from churn is $[X] ([Y] accounts). Net revenue retention is [Z]%."
- "The revenue impact of improving [metric] by [X] points is approximately $[Y] per [period]."
- "Pricing tier analysis: [tier A] customers generate $[X] ARPU vs $[Y] for [tier B], but [tier B] has [Z]x the volume."

## Referral Lever

- "Referral-driven signups accounted for [X]% of new users this [period], [up/down] from [Y]%."
- "The referral program generated [X] new users at a cost of $[Y] per referred user, compared to $[Z] CAC for paid channels."
- "Users acquired through referral retain at [X]%, which is [Y] points higher than the average across all channels."
- "Viral coefficient is [X]. Values above 1.0 indicate self-sustaining growth; we are currently [above/below] that threshold."
- "The most common referral path is [description]. [X]% of referrals convert within [Y] days."
- "NPS promoters (score 9-10) refer at [X]x the rate of passives. Growing the promoter segment is a key referral lever."

## Cross-Lever Insights

- "The highest-impact lever right now is [lever], where a [X]% improvement would yield [Y] in [business outcome]."
- "Acquisition is driving volume, but the growth model is constrained by [activation/retention], which is leaking [X]% of acquired users."
- "The most efficient growth path is [specific combination], which compounds [lever A] and [lever B]."
- "We are approaching saturation on [lever]. Shifting focus to [alternative lever] is expected to yield better marginal returns."
