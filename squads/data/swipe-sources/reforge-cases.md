# Reforge: Growth and Retention Case Studies

Index of Reforge's most valuable frameworks and case studies for growth, retention, and monetization.

---

## Growth Frameworks

- **"Growth Loops vs. Funnels"** (Andrew Chen, Brian Balfour) - Why linear funnels are the wrong mental model for growth. Introduces growth loops: the compounding systems where one cohort of users generates the next. Case studies: Pinterest's SEO content loop, Slack's viral team-invite loop, and HubSpot's content-to-lead loop.
- **"The Four Growth Frameworks"** - Systematic categorization of growth models: viral (user-invite-user), content (SEO/UGC), paid (scalable acquisition), and sales (enterprise). How to identify which framework fits your product.
- **"Growth Model Audit"** - How to map your company's growth model, identify the binding constraint, and prioritize efforts. Includes a step-by-step growth model worksheet used by Reforge portfolio companies.

## Retention Deep-Dives

- **"Retention is King"** (Brian Balfour) - Quantitative argument for why retention trumps acquisition. A 5% improvement in retention is worth 25-95% more in LTV depending on the business model. Case studies from HubSpot and Bumble.
- **"The Retention Lifecycle Framework"** - Detailed breakdown of three retention phases: new user (days 0-14), current user (weeks 2-12), and resurrected user (12+ weeks dormant). Different strategies and metrics for each phase.
- **"Activation Energy"** (Casey Winters) - The concept of "activation energy" in onboarding: the minimum investment a user must make before the product delivers value. Case studies: Grammarly (low activation energy, fast value), Figma (medium, requires team adoption), and Salesforce (high, requires data migration).

## Monetization

- **"Pricing and Packaging Frameworks"** - How to structure pricing tiers using value metric analysis. Case studies: Slack (per-active-user pricing), Zoom (per-host pricing), and Notion (per-member pricing). How each company's value metric aligned with their growth model.
- **"The Expansion Revenue Playbook"** - Strategies for driving NRR above 100%: usage-based expansion, seat-based expansion, and cross-sell. Quantitative benchmarks by company stage and business model.
- **"Free-to-Paid Conversion Optimization"** - Framework for optimizing the free-to-paid conversion in freemium products. Covers: free tier design, conversion triggers, pricing page optimization, and trial mechanics.

## Engagement

- **"The Engagement Framework"** (Casey Winters) - How to measure and improve product engagement using habit loops. Covers trigger design, core action optimization, and reward mechanisms. Case studies from Instagram, Duolingo, and Slack.
- **"Building Habit-Forming Products"** - Intersection of product design and behavioral science. How Spotify's Discover Weekly, Netflix's auto-play, and Duolingo's streaks create habitual engagement.

## Network Effects and Marketplaces

- **"Marketplace Growth and Liquidity"** - Framework for growing two-sided marketplaces: achieving liquidity (sufficient supply-demand matching), scaling beyond initial markets, and maintaining marketplace quality. Case studies: Airbnb, Uber, and Etsy.
- **"Network Effects: Types and Strategies"** - Taxonomy of network effects (direct, indirect, data, platform) with strategies for building and defending each type. Case studies from LinkedIn, Figma, and Shopify.

## Product Strategy

- **"Product-Market Fit Measurement"** - Moving beyond the "40% very disappointed" survey. Quantitative methods for measuring and tracking PMF using retention curves, engagement depth, and organic growth rates.
- **"Feature Prioritization at Scale"** - Frameworks for prioritizing features when you have more ideas than capacity: ICE scoring, RICE scoring, and opportunity scoring. When each framework works best.

## Organizational Frameworks

- **"Building a Growth Team"** - Org design for growth teams: where they sit, what they own, how they interact with product and marketing. Mistakes to avoid and milestones for scaling.
- **"Data-Informed vs. Data-Driven"** - When to use data to inform decisions vs. when to rely on judgment. Framework for matching decision type to analytical rigor required.
