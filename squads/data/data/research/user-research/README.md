# User Research

This subdirectory contains summaries and findings from user research activities including interviews, surveys, and usability studies.

## Purpose

Quantitative data tells us what is happening; user research tells us why. This directory bridges the gap between behavioral metrics and user motivations, providing the qualitative context needed to interpret data correctly and design effective interventions.

## What Goes Here

- User interview summaries with key themes and quotes
- Survey results and analysis (NPS, CSAT, custom surveys)
- Usability study findings and task success rates
- Jobs-to-be-done research and persona validation
- Synthesis documents that combine multiple research inputs into actionable insights
- Research-informed hypotheses that feed into the experiment backlog

## Conventions

- Name files with a date and research type: `2026-02-churn-interview-synthesis.md`
- Anonymize all user data; never include personally identifiable information
- Structure each document with a summary at the top, methodology, findings, and recommended actions
- Tag findings with the metrics or segments they relate to for cross-referencing
- Link to any experiments that were designed based on the research findings
- Archive outdated research rather than deleting it, as historical context has long-term value
