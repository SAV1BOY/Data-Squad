# Customer Success Metrics

This subdirectory contains analyses and documentation for customer success metrics including health scores, satisfaction, and support effectiveness.

## Purpose

Customer success metrics track the health and satisfaction of the customer base, with a focus on identifying at-risk accounts, measuring support quality, and ensuring customers achieve their goals with the product.

## What Goes Here

- Health score distribution analyses and trend tracking
- CSAT and NPS survey results and trend analyses
- Support ticket volume, resolution time, and category analyses
- Account health deep-dives for strategic accounts
- CS team performance and capacity analyses
- Correlation studies between health signals and churn outcomes

## Conventions

- Name files with the metric area and period: `health-score-distribution-feb-2026.md`
- Reference the health score model version being used (see cs-health-registry.yaml)
- Track both aggregate health trends and the tails of the distribution (critical accounts)
- Include actionable recommendations for the CS team when presenting analyses
- Coordinate with CS leadership on reporting cadence and format preferences
