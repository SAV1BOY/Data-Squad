# Activation Loop Debug

## Purpose

Debug the activation loop: the critical path from signup to the "aha moment" where the user first experiences core product value. Ellis identifies activation as the highest-leverage growth lever because it multiplies the value of every acquired user. This checklist systematically identifies and addresses friction in the activation path.

---

## Checklist Items

### 1. The "Aha Moment" Is Defined and Validated
- **Pass:** The team has identified the specific in-product action that correlates with long-term retention (e.g., "added 3 teammates within 7 days"). The correlation has been validated with data.
- **Fail:** The aha moment is assumed ("they probably get value when they...") but never validated with retention cohort analysis.

### 2. Time-to-Value Is Measured
- **Pass:** The median time from signup to aha moment is tracked, trended, and actively minimized. The distribution is examined, not just the median.
- **Fail:** Nobody knows how long it takes a new user to reach the aha moment.

### 3. The Activation Funnel Is Mapped Step by Step
- **Pass:** Every step from signup to aha moment is mapped with conversion rate, drop-off rate, and median time at each step. The biggest drop-off points are identified.
- **Fail:** Activation is a single conversion rate (signup to "activated") with no visibility into where users get stuck.

### 4. Friction Points Are Identified and Prioritized
- **Pass:** The top 3 friction points in the activation funnel are documented with hypotheses for why users drop off at each point.
- **Fail:** Drop-off points are visible in the data but nobody has investigated why users leave.

### 5. Onboarding Guides Users to the Aha Moment
- **Pass:** The onboarding flow is explicitly designed to move users toward the aha moment as quickly as possible. Each onboarding step has a clear purpose in this journey.
- **Fail:** Onboarding is a generic product tour or feature walkthrough with no connection to the validated aha moment.

### 6. Empty States Are Resolved
- **Pass:** When a new user encounters an empty screen (no data, no content, no connections), there is a meaningful prompt, sample data, or guided action to keep momentum.
- **Fail:** New users hit blank screens with no guidance, leading to confusion and abandonment.

### 7. Activation Rate Is Segmented
- **Pass:** Activation rate is broken down by acquisition channel, user persona, and device to identify which segments activate well and which struggle.
- **Fail:** Only an aggregate activation rate is reported, hiding segment-level problems.

### 8. Re-Engagement for Non-Activated Users Exists
- **Pass:** Users who sign up but do not reach the aha moment within a defined window receive targeted re-engagement (email, in-app, push) with specific nudges toward activation.
- **Fail:** Non-activated users are abandoned -- they either figure it out or they are lost.

### 9. Activation Experiments Are Running Continuously
- **Pass:** There are always 1-2 experiments in flight specifically targeting activation improvement. Activation is a standing item in the growth backlog.
- **Fail:** Activation was "fixed" once and is no longer actively experimented on.

### 10. Activation Loops Feed Growth Loops
- **Pass:** The activation flow includes natural hooks for virality or network effects (invite teammates, share a result, connect with others) that create a growth loop.
- **Fail:** Activation is a dead end -- the user activates but there is no mechanism for them to bring others into the product.
