# See-Think-Do-Care Mapping

## Purpose

Validate that the organization's digital strategy maps content, metrics, and tracking to each stage of Kaushik's See-Think-Do-Care framework. Each stage represents a distinct audience intent cluster, and treating them identically is a common and expensive mistake.

---

## Checklist Items

### 1. All Four Stages Are Explicitly Defined
- **Pass:** SEE (largest addressable qualified audience), THINK (audience with some commercial intent), DO (audience with strong commercial intent), and CARE (existing customers) are each documented with audience definitions.
- **Fail:** One or more stages are missing, or stages are defined only as funnel labels without audience descriptions.

### 2. Content Exists for Each Stage
- **Pass:** There is at least one content type mapped to each stage (e.g., SEE = blog/video, THINK = comparison guides, DO = product pages, CARE = loyalty content).
- **Fail:** Content is concentrated on DO stage only, with SEE and CARE neglected.

### 3. Metrics Are Stage-Appropriate
- **Pass:** SEE uses awareness metrics (reach, impressions, brand recall). THINK uses consideration metrics (engagement depth, return visits). DO uses conversion metrics (transactions, revenue). CARE uses loyalty metrics (repeat purchase rate, NPS, LTV).
- **Fail:** Conversion rate is applied to SEE content, or awareness metrics are used to judge DO campaigns.

### 4. Tracking Is Instrumented per Stage
- **Pass:** Analytics tooling captures stage-specific events and dimensions (e.g., UTM parameters, custom dimensions, event categories) that allow filtering by stage.
- **Fail:** All traffic is pooled without the ability to isolate stage-specific behavior.

### 5. Paid Media Aligns to Stage Intent
- **Pass:** Ad campaigns are tagged and measured by STDC stage. Display/video budgets are evaluated with SEE metrics, not DO metrics.
- **Fail:** All paid media is measured by last-click conversion regardless of campaign intent.

### 6. No Stage Is Measured by the Wrong Success Criteria
- **Pass:** Each stage's report leads with its own success criteria. SEE reports do not headline with revenue; DO reports do not headline with impressions.
- **Fail:** A single dashboard applies uniform KPIs across all stages.

### 7. CARE Stage Has a Defined Program
- **Pass:** There is a documented program for existing customers (email nurture, community, loyalty program) with its own budget and metrics.
- **Fail:** CARE is assumed to happen automatically or is entirely absent from strategy documents.

### 8. Stage Transitions Are Tracked
- **Pass:** Movement from one stage to the next is measurable (e.g., a SEE visitor who returns and enters THINK, or a THINK visitor who converts to DO).
- **Fail:** Stages exist in theory but there is no mechanism to observe transitions between them.

### 9. Budget Allocation Reflects Stage Portfolio
- **Pass:** Marketing budget is distributed across stages with documented rationale. Overinvestment in DO at the expense of SEE/THINK is flagged.
- **Fail:** 90%+ of budget goes to DO-stage activities with no discussion of portfolio balance.

### 10. Content Gaps per Stage Are Identified
- **Pass:** A content gap analysis has been performed for each stage in the last 6 months, with a backlog of content to fill gaps.
- **Fail:** No gap analysis exists, or content production is reactive with no stage-aware planning.
