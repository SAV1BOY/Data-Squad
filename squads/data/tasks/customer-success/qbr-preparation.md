# Task: QBR Preparation
> Agent(s): Customer Success Analyst, Data Analyst
> Frameworks: Quarterly Business Review Framework, Data Storytelling
> Checklists: qbr-preparation-checklist, narrative-quality-checklist
> Template: qbr-template
> Registry: analysis-registry

## Objective
Prepare a data-driven Quarterly Business Review (QBR) package for a strategic customer
account, summarizing their usage, value realized, success metrics, and recommendations
for the next quarter to strengthen the relationship and drive expansion.

## Prerequisites
- Account is identified and QBR date is scheduled.
- Access to account-level usage, adoption, and revenue data.
- Customer success plan with documented goals (if available).
- CSM input on qualitative account status.

## Steps
1. **Gather account context** -- Review:
   - Contract details (start date, renewal date, ARR, plan type).
   - Original goals and success criteria from the CS plan.
   - Notes from previous QBR (if any).
   - CSM relationship notes and recent interactions.
2. **Pull usage data** -- Extract the account's product usage for the quarter:
   - Active users vs. licensed seats (adoption rate).
   - Feature usage breadth and depth.
   - Login frequency trends.
   - Key action completion rates.
3. **Calculate value metrics** -- Quantify the value the customer received:
   - Time saved (estimated from workflow automation).
   - Revenue influenced (if attribution is available).
   - Efficiency gains (before/after comparisons if available).
   Map to the customer's stated goals.
4. **Benchmark usage** -- Compare the account's usage to:
   - Peer accounts (same size, industry, plan).
   - Best-in-class accounts.
   Highlight areas where they are ahead or behind peers.
5. **Identify success highlights** -- Find 3-5 positive stories:
   - Features adopted this quarter.
   - Usage growth trends.
   - User milestones (100th user, 1000th action, etc.).
   - ROI achieved against original goals.
6. **Identify improvement areas** -- Find 2-3 areas for improvement:
   - Features not yet adopted that would add value.
   - Declining usage trends.
   - Low adoption by certain teams or roles.
   Frame as opportunities, not criticisms.
7. **Prepare recommendations** -- For each improvement area, prepare a specific
   recommendation with:
   - What to do (training, feature enablement, workflow change).
   - Expected impact.
   - Suggested timeline.
8. **Build the QBR deck** -- Create the presentation:
   - Slide 1: Agenda and relationship summary.
   - Slide 2-3: Usage and adoption highlights.
   - Slide 4: Value delivered (mapped to goals).
   - Slide 5: Peer benchmarking.
   - Slide 6: Improvement opportunities.
   - Slide 7: Recommendations for next quarter.
   - Slide 8: Roadmap preview (new features relevant to this customer).
   - Slide 9: Next steps and action items.
9. **Prepare for tough questions** -- Anticipate customer concerns:
   - Feature requests that have not been delivered.
   - Known bugs or issues affecting the account.
   - Pricing or contract questions.
   Prepare data-backed responses.
10. **Review with CSM** -- Walk through the QBR package with the assigned CSM. Incorporate
    their qualitative insights and relationship context.
11. **Finalize and deliver** -- Polish the deck and share with the customer at least
    24 hours before the meeting (if sharing pre-read is expected).

## Deliverable
A QBR package containing:
- QBR slide deck (8-12 slides).
- Usage and adoption data summary.
- Value realization metrics mapped to customer goals.
- Peer benchmarking comparison.
- Recommendations for next quarter.
- Appendix with detailed data tables.

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Account context is fully gathered (contract, goals, history).
- [ ] Usage data covers the full quarter.
- [ ] Value metrics are mapped to the customer's stated goals.
- [ ] Peer benchmarking is included with at least 5 comparison accounts.
- [ ] 3-5 success highlights are documented.
- [ ] 2-3 improvement areas with specific recommendations.
- [ ] QBR deck is reviewed by the CSM.
- [ ] Tough questions are anticipated with prepared responses.
