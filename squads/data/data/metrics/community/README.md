# Community Metrics

This subdirectory contains analyses and documentation for community engagement, growth, and health metrics.

## Purpose

Community metrics track the vitality and effectiveness of the community as a product extension. A healthy community reduces support costs, increases retention, and creates a competitive moat through network effects.

## What Goes Here

- Community engagement score tracking and decomposition
- Member growth and activity trend analyses
- Content contribution and consumption ratio analyses
- Community loop health assessments (see community-registry.yaml)
- Community-to-product correlation studies (does community participation predict retention?)
- Peer support effectiveness analyses (questions resolved by community vs. support team)

## Conventions

- Name files with the analysis focus and date: `community-engagement-feb-2026.md`
- Note data quality limitations for community analytics (see data-quality-registry.yaml)
- Track both leading indicators (posts, replies) and lagging indicators (member retention)
- Compare community participants vs. non-participants on key product metrics
- Coordinate with the community lead on interpretation and action items
