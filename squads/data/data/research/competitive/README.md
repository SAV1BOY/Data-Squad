# Competitive Research

This subdirectory contains competitive intelligence on how comparable products measure, report, and perform.

## Purpose

Understanding the competitive landscape helps the data team benchmark performance, identify industry-standard metrics, and anticipate market shifts. This directory stores structured competitive analysis that informs metric design and strategic decisions.

## What Goes Here

- Competitor product analyses focusing on data and analytics capabilities
- Public metric disclosures from competitor earnings calls, blog posts, or press releases
- Feature comparison matrices relevant to engagement and retention drivers
- Competitive positioning assessments that inform metric prioritization
- Market share and growth rate estimates from public or syndicated sources

## Conventions

- Name files with the competitor or topic and date: `2026-02-competitor-x-metrics-review.md`
- Use only publicly available information; do not store anything obtained through non-public channels
- Focus on data-relevant aspects: what they measure, how they define metrics, what they report publicly
- Update competitive files quarterly or when significant new information becomes available
- Summarize implications for our own metric strategy at the end of each document
- Flag any competitive changes that could affect our market benchmarks or definitions
