# Task: Champion Impact Analysis
> Agent(s): Community Analyst, Data Analyst
> Frameworks: Champion Impact Framework, Influence Network Analysis
> Checklists: champion-analysis-checklist, analysis-rigor-checklist
> Template: champion-impact-template
> Registry: analysis-registry, metric-registry

## Objective
Measure the outsized impact of community champions (top contributors) on community health,
member success, and business outcomes to justify investment in champion programs and optimize
champion cultivation strategies.

## Prerequisites
- Champion definition is established (criteria for who qualifies).
- Community activity data at the member level.
- Ability to track champion interactions with other members.
- Business data (retention, revenue) joinable to community data.

## Steps
1. **Define champions** -- Formalize the champion criteria:
   - Quantitative: top X% by posts, answers, helpful votes.
   - Qualitative: recognized by community team, consistent quality.
   - Tenure: minimum time in community (e.g., 3+ months active).
   Document the criteria and the current champion count.
2. **Profile champions** -- Characterize the champion cohort:
   - Demographics: role, company size, industry, geography.
   - Product usage: plan type, feature adoption, engagement level.
   - Community tenure: how long they have been active.
   - Contribution patterns: content types, topics, frequency.
3. **Measure content impact** -- For champion-created content:
   - View count vs. average member content.
   - Engagement rate (replies, likes) vs. average.
   - Answer quality: accepted answer rate, helpful vote rate.
   - Content lifespan (how long it continues to get views).
4. **Measure support impact** -- Quantify champion contribution to community support:
   - Questions answered by champions / total answered questions.
   - Average response time for champion answers vs. staff answers.
   - Estimated support tickets deflected by champion answers.
   - Dollar value of deflection (tickets deflected x cost per ticket).
5. **Measure influence on other members** -- Analyze champion effect on member behavior:
   - Do members who interact with champions retain better?
   - Do members who receive champion answers become more active?
   - Do champions inspire other members to contribute?
   Track the "ripple effect" of champion activity.
6. **Measure business outcomes** -- Compare champion accounts:
   - Retention rate for champion accounts vs. non-champion accounts.
   - Expansion rate for champion accounts.
   - Referral rate from champions.
   Control for selection bias using matching (champions are inherently more engaged).
7. **Calculate champion ROI** -- Estimate the total value generated by champions:
   - Support deflection value.
   - Content value (views, engagement).
   - Retention premium for their accounts.
   - Referral value.
   Compare to the cost of the champion program.
8. **Analyze champion lifecycle** -- Track:
   - Path to becoming a champion (how long, what triggers).
   - Champion retention rate (how long they stay active).
   - Champion burnout signals (activity decline).
   - Succession planning: who is the next generation of champions?
9. **Identify champion cultivation opportunities** -- Find members who are near-champions:
   - High activity but not yet at champion level.
   - High quality but low volume.
   - New members with rapid engagement growth.
10. **Recommend program improvements** -- Based on findings, suggest:
    - Recognition and reward adjustments.
    - Support and enablement for champions.
    - Burnout prevention strategies.
    - Cultivation tactics for near-champions.
11. **Document and present** -- Create the champion impact report.

## Deliverable
A champion impact analysis report containing:
- Champion definition and current cohort profile.
- Content impact metrics (views, engagement, quality).
- Support impact metrics (deflection value, response times).
- Influence analysis (ripple effects on other members).
- Business outcome comparison (with bias controls).
- Champion ROI calculation.
- Lifecycle analysis (path, retention, burnout).
- Cultivation opportunities and program recommendations.

## Quality Gate
- [ ] Champion criteria are formally defined and documented.
- [ ] Content impact is measured with comparison to non-champion content.
- [ ] Support deflection value is estimated in dollar terms.
- [ ] Influence on other members is analyzed.
- [ ] Business outcomes control for selection bias.
- [ ] Champion ROI is calculated with program costs.
- [ ] Lifecycle analysis covers path, retention, and burnout.
- [ ] Near-champion cultivation opportunities are identified.
