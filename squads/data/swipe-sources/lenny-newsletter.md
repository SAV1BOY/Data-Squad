# Lenny's Newsletter: Growth Metrics Articles

Index of the most valuable articles from Lenny Rachitsky's newsletter on growth, product, and metrics.

---

## Metrics and Benchmarks

- **"What is a Good Retention Rate?"** - Comprehensive benchmark data for retention rates across consumer social (Day 1: 40-60%, Day 30: 15-25%), consumer transactional (Month 1: 30-50%), SaaS (Month 1: 80-90%), and marketplace (Month 1: 20-40%) products. The most cited retention benchmark article in the industry.
- **"What is a Good Activation Rate?"** - Benchmarks for activation rates by product type: consumer apps (20-40%), SaaS (30-50% of sign-ups complete onboarding), and PLG products (15-30% free-to-paid). Includes how top companies define activation.
- **"What is a Good NRR?"** - Benchmark data: median SaaS NRR is 100-110%. Best-in-class is 130%+. Breaks down by company stage and segment (SMB vs. enterprise). Covers the math of why NRR above 100% creates compounding growth.
- **"What is a Good Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate?"** - Benchmarks: median for freemium SaaS is 2-5%. Best performers (Slack, Zoom, Dropbox) achieve 5-10%. Reverse-trial models achieve 8-15%. Includes strategies for improving conversion.
- **"What is Good Engagement? DAU/MAU Benchmarks"** - Consumer social: 50%+ DAU/MAU is excellent. Productivity tools: 30-40%. E-commerce: 10-20%. How to interpret DAU/MAU for different product types.

## Growth Frameworks

- **"How the Biggest Consumer Apps Got Their First 1,000 Users"** - Case studies of initial growth strategies: Tinder (campus parties), Uber (black car service at events), Instagram (photographer community), Notion (template creators). Categorized by strategy type: supply-first, community, content, paid.
- **"The Most Popular Growth Frameworks"** - Survey of growth leaders on which frameworks they actually use: growth loops (48%), AARRR pirate metrics (35%), north star metric (31%), jobs-to-be-done (28%). Pros and cons of each.
- **"Choosing Your North Star Metric"** - Decision framework for selecting a north star metric. Categories: attention (time spent), transaction (revenue), productivity (tasks completed). Why you should have only one, and how to decompose it into input metrics.

## Retention Deep-Dives

- **"How to Improve Your Retention"** - Tactical playbook covering: onboarding optimization, notification strategy, re-engagement campaigns, feature development for retained users, and pricing changes that improve retention.
- **"The Adjacent User Theory"** (Bangaly Kaba, ex-Instagram) - How to grow by identifying and serving "adjacent users" -- people who are one step away from being power users. Framework for mapping the user journey and identifying where adjacent users get stuck.
- **"Why Your Retention Curve Hasn't Flattened"** - Diagnostic framework: if your retention curve never flattens, you have an activation problem. If it flattens but at a low rate, you have an engagement problem. Different solutions for each.

## Product-Led Growth

- **"The PLG Metrics Stack"** - Complete metrics framework for PLG companies: sign-up rate, activation rate, engagement rate, PQL rate, self-serve conversion, and expansion rate. Benchmarks for each.
- **"How to Build a PQL Model"** - Practical guide to defining and scoring Product Qualified Leads. Case studies from Figma, Notion, and Miro showing their PQL criteria and conversion rates.
- **"Virality vs. Word of Mouth vs. Network Effects"** - Clarifying three commonly confused concepts. Virality (K-factor), word of mouth (NPS-driven referral), and network effects (value increases with users). Different strategies for each.

## Pricing and Monetization

- **"How to Price Your Product"** - Step-by-step pricing framework: identify value metric, research willingness-to-pay, structure tiers, and test pricing changes. Case studies from Figma, Linear, and Notion.
- **"Reverse Trials: The Growth Hack Everyone is Copying"** - Why starting users on a full-featured trial (then downgrading to free) outperforms traditional freemium. Case studies: Airtable, Loom, and Miro.

## Guest Expert Posts

- **"How Duolingo Reignited User Growth"** (Jorge Mazal) - Detailed case study of Duolingo's growth turnaround: the "current user retention" focus, notification optimization, streak mechanics, and leaderboards. Includes specific metric improvements.
- **"How Figma Grows"** (Claire Butler) - Inside Figma's growth model: bottom-up adoption, viral design sharing, community templates, and the enterprise expansion playbook.
