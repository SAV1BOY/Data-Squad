# Amplitude Blog: Best Articles on Product Analytics

Index of valuable Amplitude blog content for product analytics practitioners.

---

## Foundational Product Analytics

- **"The North Star Metric"** - Framework for identifying the single metric that best captures the core value a product delivers. Covers how Spotify (time spent listening), Airbnb (nights booked), and Facebook (DAU) chose their north stars. Includes a worksheet for deriving your own.
- **"Product Analytics Playbook"** - Comprehensive guide covering event taxonomy design, user identity resolution, and building your first analytics stack. Best starting point for teams new to product analytics.
- **"Behavioral Cohorts: The Missing Piece"** - How to segment users by what they do (not who they are) and why behavioral cohorts outperform demographic segments for predicting retention and conversion.

## Activation and Onboarding

- **"Finding Your Product's Aha Moment"** - Step-by-step methodology for identifying the activation event that predicts long-term retention. Uses Slack's "2000 messages" and Dropbox's "1 file in 1 folder on 1 device" as case studies. Includes SQL templates.
- **"Measuring Activation: Beyond the Funnel"** - Why linear funnels miss non-linear activation paths. Introduces the "activation surface" concept where multiple feature combinations can indicate activation.
- **"Time to Value: The Most Underrated Metric"** - Research showing that time-to-first-value is a stronger retention predictor than feature usage volume. Benchmarks for SaaS (median: 3.5 days) and consumer apps (median: 2 sessions).

## Retention and Engagement

- **"Retention Lifecycle Framework"** - Breaks retention into three phases (new user retention, current user retention, resurrected user retention) with different strategies for each. Based on analysis of 12,000 products on Amplitude's platform.
- **"Building a Retention Model That Actually Works"** - Technical guide to building retention prediction models from product usage data. Covers feature engineering, model selection, and integration with CS/marketing tools.
- **"Engagement Metrics That Matter"** - Comparison of DAU/MAU, L7/L28, and stickiness metrics. Explains when each is appropriate and how to benchmark against industry averages.

## Experimentation

- **"Statistical Significance is Not Enough"** - Why teams should also consider practical significance, segment effects, and long-term impact. Includes a decision framework for shipping experiments.
- **"Building an Experiment Culture"** - Organizational guide to scaling experimentation from ad-hoc tests to a systematic program. Covers experiment velocity, learning documentation, and team structure.

## Advanced Topics

- **"Product-Led Growth Metrics Stack"** - Complete metrics framework for PLG companies: acquisition loop metrics, activation metrics, PQL scoring, expansion signals, and viral coefficient tracking.
- **"The Impact Matrix: Prioritizing What to Measure"** - Framework for deciding which metrics to invest in tracking, based on decision impact and measurement difficulty.
- **"Data Governance for Product Analytics"** - Practical guide to event naming conventions, data quality monitoring, and taxonomy management at scale.

## Best Practice Guides

- **"Amplitude Academy"** - Free structured learning path covering product analytics fundamentals, intermediate analysis techniques, and advanced behavioral analysis.
- **"Taxonomy Guide"** - Comprehensive event naming conventions and tracking plan templates. Includes examples from e-commerce, SaaS, and marketplace products.
