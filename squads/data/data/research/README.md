# Research

This directory contains all research materials that inform data strategy, metric design, and product analytics decisions.

## Purpose

Research is the foundation for evidence-based decision-making. This directory serves as the central repository for external and internal research that the data team references when defining metrics, designing experiments, building models, or advising stakeholders.

## What Goes Here

- Market benchmarks and industry data relevant to our product category
- User research summaries, interview notes, and survey analysis
- Competitive intelligence on how comparable products measure and report
- Platform and ecosystem change tracking (e.g., app store policy changes, browser privacy updates)
- Methodology notes explaining analytical approaches and statistical techniques used by the team

## Subdirectories

- `market-benchmarks/` - Industry benchmarks, SaaS metrics reports, analyst data
- `user-research/` - User interview summaries, survey results, UX research findings
- `competitive/` - Competitor analysis, feature comparisons, public metric disclosures
- `platform-changes/` - Tracking changes to platforms, APIs, or regulations that affect data collection
- `methodology-notes/` - Internal documentation of statistical methods, sampling approaches, and analytical frameworks

## Conventions

- Name files descriptively with a date prefix: `2026-02-market-benchmark-saas-retention.md`
- Include the source and retrieval date for any external data
- Mark any confidential or proprietary information clearly in the file header
- Summarize key takeaways at the top of each document so readers can quickly assess relevance
- Link back to any decisions or metrics that were influenced by the research
