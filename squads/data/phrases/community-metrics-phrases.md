# Community Metrics Phrases

## Purpose

Standardized phrases for communicating community engagement, health, and growth metrics. Designed to respect the human nature of community while maintaining analytical rigor.

## Reporting Community Size and Growth

- "The community grew to [X] members this [period], an increase of [Y]% from [prior period]."
- "[X] new members joined this [period], with [Y]% completing their first interaction within 30 days."
- "Net community growth was [X] members ([Y] joined, [Z] became inactive)."
- "The community has grown [X]% year over year, with the strongest growth in [category/region/topic area]."
- "New member acquisition is primarily driven by [channel/source], which accounts for [X]% of new joins."

## Reporting Engagement

- "[X]% of community members were active this [period], defined as posting, commenting, or reacting at least once."
- "Discussion volume [increased/decreased] [X]% this [period], with [Y] new threads and [Z] total replies."
- "Average thread depth (replies per discussion) was [X], [up/down] from [Y] in the prior [period]."
- "The most active discussion topics were [topic 1], [topic 2], and [topic 3], collectively accounting for [X]% of all activity."
- "Community members contributed [X] pieces of content this [period], including [breakdown by type]."
- "Time to first response averaged [X] hours this [period], [meeting/exceeding/missing] our target of [Y] hours."

## Describing Member Segments

- "Active contributors (those who post or reply at least [X] times per month) make up [Y]% of the community and drive [Z]% of content."
- "Community readers (members who view content but do not post) represent [X]% of the membership."
- "Returning contributors (active in 2+ consecutive months) increased to [X], suggesting deepening engagement."
- "[X] members qualify as community champions based on [criteria: frequency, helpfulness ratings, content quality]."
- "New member activation (first post within 30 days of joining) is at [X]%, [up/down] from [Y]%."

## Reporting Community Health

- "Overall community health is [strong/stable/needs attention], based on [key indicators]."
- "Engagement depth is [improving/stable/declining]: members are [spending more time / writing longer responses / participating in more categories]."
- "The ratio of questions answered to questions asked is [X]%, indicating a [healthy/struggling] self-serve knowledge ecosystem."
- "Sentiment in community discussions is [predominantly positive / mixed / showing signs of frustration in specific areas]."
- "Community satisfaction, as measured by [survey/poll], is at [X] out of [Y], [trending direction]."

## Connecting Community to Business

- "Community members retain at [X]% versus [Y]% for non-community members, a [Z] percentage point difference."
- "Accounts with active community participants have [X]% higher NPS on average."
- "Community-sourced feature requests influenced [X] shipped features this quarter."
- "[X]% of support questions were resolved by community peers, reducing support ticket volume by an estimated [Y]%."
- "Community engagement correlates with product adoption: active community members use [X] more features on average."

## Celebrating Milestones

- "The community reached [milestone] this [period], a testament to the members who contribute every day."
- "This quarter, [X] community members earned [achievement/badge/recognition], up from [Y] last quarter."
- "Highlight: [anonymized example of community member helping another], illustrating the community's culture of support."
- "The community's top discussion this [period] received [X] replies and helped [Y] members solve [problem]."

## Reporting Challenges

- "Engagement in [category/region] has declined [X]% over [period]. We are investigating potential causes."
- "New member activation is below target. The drop-off point is [specific stage in the onboarding journey]."
- "Spam and low-quality posts increased [X]% this [period]. Moderation response time was [Y] hours."
- "Community sentiment around [topic] has turned negative. [X] threads in the past [period] express frustration with [specific issue]."
