# Customer Health Communication Phrases

## Purpose

Standardized phrases for communicating customer health status, risk signals, and opportunity indicators to CS teams and, where appropriate, to customers.

## Describing Healthy Accounts

- "This account is in strong health: usage is [X]% above segment average, with [Y] active users and consistent login frequency."
- "All health indicators are positive. Feature adoption is broad, and engagement has been stable for [X] months."
- "This account is a strong candidate for expansion based on [usage pattern / seat utilization / feature ceiling]."
- "Health score: green. No action needed at this time. Next review: [date]."
- "The customer's team is actively using [X] of [Y] available features, placing them in the top quartile for their segment."
- "Login frequency and session depth are both trending up. This is a healthy engagement pattern."

## Flagging Risk Signals

- "This account is showing early risk signals: [specific signal, e.g., 'login frequency dropped 40% over the past 3 weeks']."
- "Health score has moved from green to yellow. Primary driver: [specific metric change]."
- "Usage patterns have shifted: [description of change]. In similar accounts, this has preceded churn [X]% of the time."
- "The account's primary champion has not logged in for [X] days. This is a leading indicator worth a check-in."
- "Feature adoption has stalled at [X] of [Y] core features. Accounts at this adoption level have a [X]% renewal rate."
- "Support ticket volume increased [X]% this month compared to their average. Sentiment in recent tickets skews negative."
- "This account is approaching renewal in [X] days with [risk factor]. Recommend proactive outreach."

## Recommending CS Actions

- "Recommended action: schedule a check-in to understand the usage drop and identify any blockers."
- "Consider offering a training session on [feature]. Accounts that adopt this feature retain at [X]% higher rates."
- "This account would benefit from an executive sponsor touchpoint given the upcoming renewal."
- "Suggest sharing a usage report highlighting their team's adoption progress and benchmarks."
- "Proactive outreach recommended. Talking points: [specific talking points based on the data]."
- "Escalation recommended: this account meets [X] of [Y] high-risk criteria. Loop in [manager/exec]."

## Customer-Facing Health Language

- "Your team's adoption of [product] has been strong, with [X] team members actively using the platform."
- "We have noticed your team is getting great value from [feature]. Teams like yours often benefit from also using [related feature]."
- "Your usage data shows an opportunity to further streamline [workflow] by leveraging [capability]."
- "Compared to similar organizations, your team is [ahead of / on par with / approaching] typical adoption milestones."
- "Here is a summary of how your team has used [product] this quarter, along with some suggestions for getting even more value."

## Renewal Context Phrases

- "Renewal is in [X] days. Current health indicators: [summary]. Risk level: [low/medium/high]."
- "Pre-renewal health check: [X] of [Y] health criteria are met. Key gap: [specific gap]."
- "This account renewed last year at [value]. Current usage supports a conversation about [expansion / same tier / risk of downgrade]."
- "Net Promoter Score from this account's most recent survey: [score]. Combined with usage data, sentiment is [positive/mixed/concerning]."

## Segment-Level Health Phrases

- "The [segment] cohort is showing [overall health assessment]. [X]% are green, [Y]% are yellow, [Z]% are red."
- "[Segment] accounts have a higher-than-average risk profile this quarter, driven by [common factor]."
- "Accounts in the [plan/tier] segment are outperforming on [metric] but underperforming on [metric]."
- "The healthiest accounts in this segment share [common characteristic]. The at-risk accounts share [different characteristic]."
