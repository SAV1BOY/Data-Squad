# Community Health Score Checklist

## Purpose

Define and measure a composite health score for the community that goes beyond vanity metrics (total members). A healthy community has active participation, diverse contributions, constructive tone, and sustainable growth. This gate ensures the team tracks what actually matters.

---

## Checklist Items

### 1. Health Score Components Are Defined and Weighted

- **Pass:** The health score combines 4-6 components: active member ratio (MAU/total members), contribution diversity (% of content from non-staff), engagement depth (replies per post, not just views), sentiment indicator, new member activation rate, and growth rate. Weights are explicit and justified.
- **Fail:** Health is measured by a single metric (member count) or an unweighted gut feeling.

### 2. Active Member Ratio Is Tracked Monthly

- **Pass:** The team distinguishes between total members, monthly active members (MAM), and contributors (members who post or reply). The ratio of MAM to total members is tracked over time with a target above 15-20%.
- **Fail:** The team celebrates "10,000 members" without knowing that only 200 are active.

### 3. Contribution Concentration Is Monitored

- **Pass:** The team measures the Gini coefficient or equivalent for contribution distribution. If the top 5% of members produce more than 80% of content, diversification initiatives are planned.
- **Fail:** A handful of super-users carry the community, and no one monitors the dependency risk.

### 4. New Member Activation Rate Is Measured

- **Pass:** The percentage of new members who make their first contribution within 30 days of joining is tracked. A benchmark exists (e.g., 25% activation), and onboarding flows are optimized to improve it.
- **Fail:** New members join and silently lurk; no one tracks whether they ever engage.

### 5. Sentiment Is Assessed Regularly

- **Pass:** A lightweight sentiment analysis (automated NLP on posts, or manual sampling of 50+ posts monthly) categorizes community tone as positive, neutral, negative, or toxic. Trends are tracked.
- **Fail:** Sentiment is assumed to be "fine" because no one has complained to the community manager directly.

### 6. Response Time and Unanswered Posts Are Tracked

- **Pass:** The median time to first response on community posts is measured. The percentage of posts that receive zero responses within 48 hours is tracked, with a target below 10%.
- **Fail:** Members post questions that go unanswered for weeks, and no one tracks this.

### 7. Health Score Is Reported and Acted On

- **Pass:** The composite health score is reported monthly to community and product leadership. Score declines trigger a review meeting with specific action items.
- **Fail:** The score is computed but lives in a dashboard no one checks.

### 8. Benchmarks Are Established Over Time

- **Pass:** At least 6 months of historical data establish the community's baseline health. Seasonal patterns (conference seasons, holidays) are accounted for. Year-over-year comparisons are possible.
- **Fail:** The health score was introduced last month and every fluctuation triggers overreaction.
