# Ambassador Program QA Checklist

## Purpose

Validate that the community ambassador (or champion/MVP) program creates genuine value for both the community and the business, with incentives that align ambassadors' interests with community health rather than creating perverse behaviors.

---

## Checklist Items

### 1. Ambassador Selection Criteria Are Transparent

- **Pass:** Selection criteria are published and based on measurable contributions: quality of answers, consistency of participation, mentoring behavior, and community standing. Selection is not based solely on volume (number of posts) or personal relationships.
- **Fail:** Ambassadors are hand-picked by the community manager without clear criteria, or selection rewards quantity over quality.

### 2. Incentives Align With Community Goals

- **Pass:** Rewards (swag, early access, conference tickets, stipends, recognition) are tied to behaviors that benefit the community: helping newcomers, writing quality content, providing product feedback. Incentives do not reward behaviors that could harm the community (e.g., volume posting to hit a quota).
- **Fail:** Ambassadors earn points per post, leading to low-quality spam to hit reward thresholds.

### 3. Ambassador Activity Is Tracked Against Expectations

- **Pass:** Each ambassador has a lightweight activity agreement (e.g., 4 quality contributions per month, respond to 2 newcomer questions per week). Actual activity is tracked and compared to expectations.
- **Fail:** Ambassadors are appointed and never held accountable; some go dormant while retaining the title.

### 4. Impact on Community Metrics Is Measured

- **Pass:** The team measures: response time on posts where ambassadors participate vs. where they do not, newcomer activation rate in ambassador-mentored cohorts vs. un-mentored, and sentiment in ambassador-led discussions.
- **Fail:** The team assumes ambassadors are valuable but has no data to support the claim.

### 5. Ambassador Feedback Loop Exists

- **Pass:** Ambassadors have a dedicated channel to provide feedback on the product, the community platform, and the program itself. Their feedback is reviewed monthly and acted upon with visible follow-up.
- **Fail:** Ambassadors are treated as unpaid labor with no voice in program design or product direction.

### 6. Diversity and Representation Are Monitored

- **Pass:** The ambassador cohort is reviewed for diversity across relevant dimensions (geography, seniority, use case, background). Under-represented groups are actively recruited.
- **Fail:** All ambassadors come from the same region, company size, or demographic, making the program unrepresentative.

### 7. Escalation Path for Ambassador Misconduct Exists

- **Pass:** A process for handling ambassador misconduct (guideline violations, abuse of privileges, conflicts of interest) is documented. It includes investigation, temporary suspension, and potential removal. The process is distinct from regular member moderation given the ambassador's elevated status.
- **Fail:** An ambassador behaves poorly and the team is paralyzed because there is no precedent or process for addressing it.

### 8. Program ROI Is Assessed Annually

- **Pass:** The total cost of the program (rewards, staff time to manage, tooling) is compared against measured benefits (support ticket deflection, content creation value, retention impact on ambassador-engaged members). The program continues only if ROI is positive or strategic value is clearly articulated.
- **Fail:** The program runs indefinitely with no assessment of whether the investment is worthwhile.
