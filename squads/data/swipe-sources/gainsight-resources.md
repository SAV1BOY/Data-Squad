# Gainsight: CS Resources and Benchmarks

Index of Gainsight's most valuable resources for customer success strategy, metrics, and operations.

---

## Health Scoring

- **"Customer Health Score 2.0"** - Comprehensive guide to building, calibrating, and operationalizing health scores. Covers signal selection, weight derivation, threshold setting, and common pitfalls. Includes benchmark data from 500+ Gainsight customers.
- **"Health Score Calibration Guide"** - How to validate and recalibrate health scores quarterly. Methodology for measuring predictive accuracy (AUC-ROC, precision/recall), adjusting weights, and handling new signals.
- **"Multi-Dimensional Health Scoring"** - Building composite scores across product usage, support sentiment, engagement, and relationship dimensions. Case studies from enterprise SaaS companies.

## NRR and Revenue Retention

- **"Net Revenue Retention Benchmarks"** - Annual benchmark report covering NRR by company size, industry, and business model. Median NRR for SaaS: 105-110%. Top quartile: 120%+. Includes breakdowns of expansion, contraction, and churn components.
- **"Expansion Playbook for CS Teams"** - How CSMs can identify and drive expansion opportunities without being "salesy." Covers usage-based signals, value realization milestones, and champion-led expansion.
- **"Churn Analysis Framework"** - Systematic methodology for analyzing churn: root cause categorization, preventability assessment, and process improvement recommendations.

## QBR and Customer Engagement

- **"The QBR Playbook"** - Complete guide to running effective QBRs: preparation (data gathering, stakeholder analysis), execution (presentation structure, facilitation), and follow-up (action item tracking, impact measurement).
- **"Executive Business Reviews"** - Elevated QBR format for C-level stakeholders: ROI quantification, strategic alignment, and forward-looking roadmap discussions. Templates included.
- **"Digital CS at Scale"** - How to deliver CS outcomes to long-tail accounts without dedicated CSMs. Covers automated health monitoring, triggered playbooks, community-led success, and one-to-many engagement.

## Customer Journey and Lifecycle

- **"Customer Journey Mapping for CS"** - How to build data-informed customer journey maps: identifying key milestones, measuring time between milestones, and detecting journey friction points.
- **"Onboarding Best Practices Report"** - Benchmark data on onboarding timelines, activation metrics, and time-to-value across 200+ SaaS companies. Median time to first value: 14 days. Top quartile: 5 days.
- **"Renewal Process Playbook"** - When to start the renewal process (benchmarks: 120 days for enterprise, 60 days for mid-market), what signals to monitor, and how to structure the renewal conversation.

## Risk Management and Early Warning

- **"Early Warning System Design"** - Framework for building proactive risk detection: signal identification, threshold setting, alert routing, and playbook triggers. Includes a maturity model from reactive to predictive.
- **"Champion Management"** - Tracking and managing your customer champions: identifying champions, measuring champion engagement, detecting champion risk (departure, role change), and building multi-threaded relationships.
- **"Escalation Management Framework"** - When and how to escalate customer issues: severity classification, escalation paths, response SLAs, and executive involvement criteria.

## Benchmarks and Reports

- **"Customer Success Index"** - Annual industry benchmark covering CS team size ratios, compensation, tool adoption, metrics tracked, and organizational structure. Based on survey of 1,000+ CS leaders.
- **"State of Customer Success Report"** - Annual trends report covering the evolution of CS from support function to revenue driver. Includes predictions for CS technology, AI adoption, and organizational changes.
- **"CS Metrics That Matter"** - Guide to the essential CS metrics: GRR, NRR, time to value, health score accuracy, CSM productivity, and customer effort score. Includes calculation methodologies and benchmarks.

## Pulse Conference Content

- **Pulse Conference Presentations** - Annual conference presentations from CS leaders at Salesforce, Adobe, Zoom, Okta, and others. Available as recordings and slides. Topics range from strategy to tactical implementation.

*Source: gainsight.com/resources, gainsight.com/blog, and Pulse conference archives. Many resources require email registration. Benchmark reports are typically gated but highly valuable.*
