# QBR Deck Outline

> Quarterly Business Review deck structure for data/analytics team presentations.
> Each section maps to 1-2 slides. Total target: 12-15 slides, 30-minute presentation.

---

## QBR: [Team Name] - [Q# YYYY]

**Presenter:** [Name, Team]
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Audience:** [e.g., "Exec team, department heads"]
**Duration:** [e.g., "30 minutes (20 present + 10 discuss)"]

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide

- Team name and quarter
- Date and presenter
- One-line theme for the quarter (e.g., "Activation Breakthrough, Retention Challenge")

_Example:_
- Data & Analytics QBR - Q4 2025
- Sarah Kim, Head of Analytics
- Theme: "Activation Gains Offset by Mid-Market Churn"

---

---

## Slide 3: Revenue and Growth Performance

**Content:**
- MRR/ARR trend (chart: monthly for the quarter + 2 prior quarters)
- New vs expansion vs contraction vs churn waterfall
- Revenue pacing against annual target

**Key Message:** [e.g., "New business is strong but net retention is dragging growth below plan"]

---

## Slide 4: Acquisition Funnel

**Content:**
- Funnel chart: Visits -> Signups -> Trials -> Activated -> Paid
- Conversion rates at each step, compared to prior quarter
- Channel mix and CAC by channel

**Key Message:** [e.g., "Activation rate improved 6pp; channel mix shifting toward higher-quality referrals"]

---

## Slide 5: Retention and Churn Deep Dive

**Content:**
- Cohort retention curves (this quarter vs prior quarters)
- Churn by segment (SMB, mid-market, enterprise)
- Top churn reasons (from surveys/interviews)

**Key Message:** [e.g., "Mid-market churn is the primary drag; root cause is addressable (integrations)"]

---

## Slide 6-7: Top 3 Strategic Insights [REQUIRED]

**One slide per insight (or combine 2-3 on one slide with supporting visuals)**

### Insight 1: [Title]
- Key data point(s)
- So what? (business implication)
- Recommended action

### Insight 2: [Title]
- Key data point(s)
- So what?
- Recommended action

### Insight 3: [Title]
- Key data point(s)
- So what?
- Recommended action

_Example:_
### Insight 1: Onboarding Redesign Drove 6pp Activation Improvement
- Activation: 28% (Q3) to 34% (Q4)
- Early cohort data shows 12% higher M2 MRR for activated users
- Ship remaining experiments, focus on time-to-value reduction

### Insight 2: Referral Is the Highest-Quality Channel at Lowest Cost
- Referral: $48 CPA, 52% higher retention, 38% higher MRR than average
- Only 10% of new customers come from referral today
- Scale referral program in Q1 with dedicated budget

---

## Slide 8: Experiment Program Results

**Content:**
- Experiments run, win rate, impact summary
- Top 3 winners with impact
- Key learnings from losses
- Pipeline for next quarter

| Stat                     | Value       |
|--------------------------|-------------|
| Experiments run          | [#]         |
| Winners                  | [#] ([%])   |
| Estimated quarterly impact| [value]    |

## Slide 9: Data Quality and Infrastructure

**Content:**
- Data quality score or summary (tracking coverage, accuracy, freshness)
- Infrastructure improvements completed
- Outstanding technical debt

**Key Message:** [e.g., "Tracking coverage at 92%, up from 78%. Two critical gaps remain."]
