# Churn Communication Phrases

## Purpose

Standardized phrases for communicating about churn: rates, risk, causes, prevention, and post-churn analysis. These phrases are designed for internal use and should be adapted before any customer-facing communication.

## Reporting Churn Rates

- "Gross churn for [period] was [X]%, representing [Y] accounts and $[Z] in lost MRR."
- "Net churn (including expansion) was [X]%, [above/below/at] our target of [Y]%."
- "Logo churn was [X]% ([Y] accounts). Revenue churn was [Z]% ($[amount])."
- "Churn rate increased [X] basis points versus the prior [period], from [old rate] to [new rate]."
- "Annualized churn is running at [X]%, which would result in [Y] lost accounts over the next 12 months at current rates."
- "Churn was concentrated in the [segment] tier, which accounted for [X]% of lost accounts but only [Y]% of lost revenue."

## Describing Churn Drivers

- "The primary driver of churn this [period] was [specific cause], accounting for [X]% of losses."
- "Exit survey data indicates [top reason] as the most cited factor, followed by [second reason]."
- "Accounts that churned shared [X] common characteristics: [list]."
- "Churn was disproportionately high among accounts that [specific behavior or attribute]."
- "The leading indicator that best predicted churn was [metric], with accounts below [threshold] churning at [X]x the average rate."
- "Competitive displacement accounted for [X]% of churn this quarter, up from [Y]% in the prior quarter."

## Communicating Churn Risk

- "There are currently [X] accounts flagged as high churn risk, representing $[Y] in ARR."
- "This account's churn probability has increased from [X]% to [Y]% based on [behavioral change]."
- "The at-risk cohort has [X] days average remaining until renewal. Intervention window is narrowing."
- "If no action is taken on the current high-risk accounts, projected churn next quarter is [X]%."
- "The risk is concentrated: [X] accounts represent [Y]% of the total at-risk revenue."

## Framing Prevention Efforts

- "Accounts that received proactive outreach when flagged as at-risk retained at [X]% compared to [Y]% without outreach."
- "The save rate for at-risk accounts this [period] was [X]%, up from [Y]% after implementing [intervention]."
- "The most effective intervention for [segment] has been [specific action], which improved retention by [X]%."
- "Early detection is working: [X]% of accounts that eventually churned were flagged [Y] days before cancellation."
- "Prevention ROI: the cost of the save program is $[X] per account versus $[Y] in revenue recovered."

## Post-Churn Analysis

- "Post-churn analysis of [period]: [X] accounts churned. [Y]% were preventable based on our assessment."
- "The most common churn path was: [step 1] followed by [step 2], leading to cancellation within [timeframe]."
- "Win-back potential: of the [X] churned accounts, [Y]% match our win-back profile based on [criteria]."
- "Accounts that churned had an average health score of [X] at the time of cancellation, versus [Y] for retained accounts."
- "The total revenue impact of churn this [period] is $[X], partially offset by $[Y] in expansion revenue."

## Sensitive Communication

- "We are seeing elevated churn in [segment]. This is a priority area for investigation and intervention."
- "While churn is above target, the trend is [improving/stable/worsening] relative to [prior period]."
- "The churn number includes [X] accounts that were expected losses due to [specific circumstance]."
- "Adjusting for known factors ([list]), the underlying churn rate is [X]%, which is [within/outside] expectations."
