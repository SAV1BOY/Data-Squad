# Community Playbooks and Resources

Index of valuable external content on community building, measurement, and ROI.

---

## Community Strategy

- **CMX Hub: "The Community Strategy Canvas"** - Framework for designing community strategy: defining purpose, identifying members, choosing platform, establishing governance, and measuring success. The most widely used community strategy template in the industry.
- **FeverBee: "The Community Strategy Framework"** - Richard Millington's comprehensive approach to community strategy: audience research, community concept, launch strategy, growth plan, and maturity model. Includes the community lifecycle model (inception, growth, maturity, mitosis).
- **"The Orbit Model"** (Orbit.love) - Alternative to traditional marketing funnels for developer and community-led growth. Maps community members by reach (audience size) and gravity (engagement depth). Useful for prioritizing community investment.

## Community Metrics and Measurement

- **CMX Hub: "Community Metrics Guide"** - Comprehensive metrics taxonomy: activity metrics (posts, replies, events), engagement metrics (DAU/MAU, response rate, depth of conversation), and outcome metrics (NPS lift, retention impact, support deflection).
- **"Measuring Community ROI"** (Common Room) - Practical guide to quantifying community impact across support deflection, product feedback value, content generation, and revenue influence. Includes calculation templates.
- **FeverBee: "Community Benchmarks Report"** - Annual benchmarks for community health: typical response rates, active member percentages, and engagement patterns by community type (support, product, practice, interest).
- **"The Community-Led Growth Model"** (Commsor) - Framework connecting community activities to business growth: community engagement drives product adoption drives retention drives advocacy drives community growth. Metrics for each stage.

## Community Operations

- **"The Community Manager's Handbook"** (CMX) - Operational guide for community managers: daily workflow, moderation policies, member escalation, content planning, and event management.
- **"Building Developer Communities"** (SlashData) - Specific to developer-focused communities: documentation contribution programs, open-source community governance, hackathon ROI measurement, and developer relations metrics.
- **"Scaling Community Programs"** (Khoros) - How to scale community operations from 1 community manager to a team: automation, member empowerment (super-user programs), and tooling recommendations.

## Community-Led Growth

- **"Community-Led Growth Playbook"** (Commsor) - How community drives acquisition, activation, and retention in PLG companies. Case studies from Notion, Figma, and dbt. Includes metrics framework for CLG.
- **"From Community to Revenue"** (Lenny's Newsletter guest post) - Case studies of companies that directly attributed revenue to community: Salesforce Trailblazer Community ($4.1M annual support deflection), HubSpot Community (14% conversion lift for active members), and Atlassian Community.
- **"The Ambassador Playbook"** (Influitive) - Building and measuring customer advocacy programs: ambassador recruitment, incentive design, impact measurement, and program scaling.

## Community Platforms and Tools

- **"Choosing a Community Platform"** (CMX) - Comparison framework for community platforms (Discourse, Circle, Khoros, Vanilla, Mighty Networks) based on use case, scale, and integration needs.
- **"Community Analytics Tools Landscape"** - Overview of analytics tools for community measurement: Common Room, Orbit, Commsor, and native platform analytics. Comparison of capabilities and pricing.

## Books and Long-Form Resources

- **"Buzzing Communities" by Richard Millington** - Foundational book on online community management. Covers community psychology, growth strategies, and engagement tactics. The most referenced book in the community management field.
- **"People Powered" by Jono Bacon** - Framework for building communities around products and companies. Covers strategy, engagement models, governance, and measurement.
- **"The Business of Belonging" by David Spinks** - How companies build community as a strategic business function. Covers organizational design, budget justification, and measuring community impact on business metrics.
