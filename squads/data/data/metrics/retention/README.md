# Retention Metrics

This subdirectory contains analyses and documentation for cohort retention, churn rates, and engagement depth metrics.

## Purpose

Retention is the clearest signal of product-market fit and long-term business health. This directory holds retention curve analyses, churn investigations, and engagement studies that help the team understand why users stay or leave.

## What Goes Here

- Cohort retention curve analyses (weekly and monthly views)
- Churn rate tracking and decomposition by segment
- Engagement depth analyses (feature breadth, session frequency, usage intensity)
- Retention benchmarking against industry standards
- Win-back and reactivation campaign analyses
- Retention driver investigations identifying what behaviors predict long-term retention

## Conventions

- Use cohort week as the standard time grain (see DEC-005)
- Name files clearly: `q1-2026-cohort-retention-curves.md`
- Always show retention curves with both absolute and indexed views
- Compare new cohorts against historical baselines to detect improvement or regression
- Include sample sizes to help readers assess statistical reliability
- Link to related churn model documentation in the models directory
