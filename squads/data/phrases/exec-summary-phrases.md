# Executive Summary Phrases

## Purpose

Ready-to-use phrases for writing executive summaries, board updates, and leadership communications. These phrases are designed to be concise, impact-oriented, and decision-ready.

## Opening with the Headline

- "The key finding this [week/month/quarter] is [one-sentence finding]."
- "In summary: [metric] [direction] by [amount], driven by [primary cause]."
- "The most important thing to know is [one-sentence takeaway]."
- "Bottom line: we are [on track / behind / ahead] on [goal], and here is why."
- "Three things to know from the data this [period]:"
- "The headline: [concise statement of the most important finding]."

## Describing Performance Against Goals

- "We are tracking at [X]% of our [quarterly/annual] target for [metric], [ahead/behind] by [amount]."
- "Current trajectory puts us at [projected value] by end of [period], versus a target of [target value]."
- "We hit our [metric] target for [period], marking [X] consecutive periods of achievement."
- "The gap between actual ([value]) and plan ([value]) widened this [period] by [amount]."
- "If current trends hold, we will [exceed/miss] the annual target by [amount/percentage]."
- "Performance is strong in [segment A] and lagging in [segment B]."

## Framing Business Impact

- "This represents approximately $[amount] in [annual/quarterly] [revenue/cost/risk]."
- "The impact translates to [X] [customers/users/accounts] affected."
- "At current run rate, this trend [adds/removes] $[amount] from [metric] per [period]."
- "The net effect on [revenue/margin/growth] is [positive/negative/neutral]."
- "This improvement is equivalent to [relatable comparison]."

## Presenting Recommendations

- "Our recommendation is [specific action], expected to [outcome] by [date]."
- "We see three options. We recommend [option] because [reason]."
- "The data supports moving forward with [initiative]. Key risk: [risk]. Mitigation: [plan]."
- "No action is needed at this time. We will continue monitoring and report back if [condition]."
- "We recommend [action]. The cost is [investment]. The expected return is [outcome]."
- "This is a decision point: [option A] optimizes for [priority A], [option B] optimizes for [priority B]."

## Communicating Risk

- "Risk to flag: [specific risk] could impact [metric] by [amount] if [condition]."
- "The primary risk is [description]. Probability: [assessment]. Impact if realized: [quantified]."
- "We have identified early warning signs in [area]. Preventive action is recommended."
- "If we do not address [issue], the projected impact by [date] is [quantified consequence]."

## Highlighting Wins

- "Notable win: [initiative] delivered [quantified result], [exceeding/meeting] expectations."
- "[Metric] reached an all-time [high/low], reflecting [cause]."
- "This quarter's [achievement] positions us well for [strategic objective]."
- "The team shipped [deliverable], which is already showing [early result]."

## Transitioning Between Topics

- "Shifting to [next topic]:"
- "On a related note, [connected finding]."
- "Separately, [unrelated but important finding]."
- "One more item for your awareness:"

## Closing an Executive Communication

- "The decision we need is: [specific decision]."
- "Next steps: [action 1] by [owner] by [date]; [action 2] by [owner] by [date]."
- "We will provide an update on this by [date]."
- "Happy to go deeper on any of these points. Detailed analysis is available at [link]."
