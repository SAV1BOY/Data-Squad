# Mixpanel Guides: Practical Analytics Tutorials

Index of Mixpanel's most useful guides and tutorials for hands-on analytics work.

---

## Getting Started

- **"The Startup's Guide to Analytics"** - Practical guide for early-stage companies on what to track, when, and why. Covers the minimum viable tracking plan, first dashboards, and avoiding over-instrumentation.
- **"Event Tracking Best Practices"** - Naming conventions, property standardization, and common tracking mistakes. Includes a decision tree for choosing between events and user properties.
- **"Data Modeling for Product Analytics"** - How to structure your event data model for flexibility and performance. Covers entity relationships, event hierarchies, and group analytics setup.

## Funnel and Conversion Analysis

- **"Funnel Analysis Playbook"** - Step-by-step guide to building, analyzing, and optimizing conversion funnels. Covers time-bounded funnels, flexible funnels, and multi-touch funnels.
- **"Conversion Rate Optimization with Data"** - How to identify the highest-leverage points in your funnel using drop-off analysis, segmentation, and correlation analysis. Includes e-commerce, SaaS, and mobile app examples.
- **"Checkout Optimization Guide"** - Specific to e-commerce: how to analyze cart abandonment, optimize checkout steps, and measure the impact of payment methods on conversion.

## Retention and Cohort Analysis

- **"Retention Analysis: The Complete Guide"** - Covers day-N retention, unbounded retention, bracket retention, and frequency retention. Explains when to use each and how to interpret curves.
- **"Cohort Analysis Tutorial"** - Hands-on walkthrough of building cohort analyses from scratch. Includes acquisition cohorts, behavioral cohorts, and cohort comparison techniques.
- **"Churn Prediction Framework"** - Using product analytics data to build simple churn prediction models without a data science team. Covers feature importance, threshold setting, and alert integration.

## User Segmentation

- **"Behavioral Segmentation Guide"** - How to create meaningful user segments from product behavior data. Covers power users, casual users, at-risk users, and feature-specific segments.
- **"Building User Personas from Data"** - Combining quantitative behavioral data with qualitative research to create data-driven personas that product teams can act on.

## Experimentation and Feature Analysis

- **"Feature Launch Analysis Template"** - How to measure the impact of a new feature: adoption curve, engagement depth, retention impact, and cannibalization effects.
- **"A/B Testing Fundamentals with Mixpanel"** - Setting up experiments, calculating sample sizes, interpreting results, and avoiding common statistical pitfalls.

## Industry-Specific Guides

- **"E-Commerce Analytics Playbook"** - Complete guide covering purchase funnel, customer lifetime value, product affinity analysis, and promotional impact measurement.
- **"SaaS Product Analytics Playbook"** - Covering free-to-paid conversion, feature adoption, team-level analytics, and expansion signal detection.
- **"Media and Entertainment Analytics"** - Content consumption patterns, recommendation effectiveness, subscription retention, and content portfolio analysis.
- **"FinTech Analytics Guide"** - User activation in financial products, transaction analysis, fraud signal detection, and regulatory compliance considerations.

## Templates and Resources

- **"KPI Dashboard Templates"** - Pre-built dashboard templates for common use cases: executive overview, growth, retention, and feature adoption.
- **"Tracking Plan Templates"** - Downloadable tracking plan spreadsheets for SaaS, e-commerce, marketplace, and mobile app products.

---

*Source: mixpanel.com/blog and mixpanel.com/content. Guides range from beginner to advanced and include interactive examples where applicable.*
