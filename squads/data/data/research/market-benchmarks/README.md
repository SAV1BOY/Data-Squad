# Market Benchmarks

This subdirectory contains industry benchmarks, SaaS metrics reports, and analyst data used to contextualize our performance.

## Purpose

Internal metrics are more meaningful when compared against industry standards. Market benchmarks provide the external reference points that help the team assess whether our performance is competitive, lagging, or leading.

## What Goes Here

- SaaS industry benchmark reports (e.g., retention benchmarks by company stage, ARPU ranges by category)
- Analyst reports and public data from firms like OpenView, ChartMogul, ProfitWell, and similar
- Aggregated benchmark data from investor presentations or earnings calls of comparable companies
- Custom benchmark compilations assembled by the data team from multiple sources
- Notes on benchmark applicability and limitations for our specific context

## Conventions

- Name files with a date prefix and topic: `2026-01-openview-saas-benchmarks.md`
- Always cite the source, publication date, and sample characteristics
- Note any caveats about comparability (e.g., different company stage, vertical, or geography)
- Highlight the specific benchmarks most relevant to our current metrics and goals
- Update or replace benchmark files annually as new data becomes available
- Do not store copyrighted full reports; summarize key data points and link to the original source
