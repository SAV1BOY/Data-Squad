## 3. Hypothesis [REQUIRED]

**If we** [describe the change or intervention],
**then** [expected measurable outcome],
**because** [evidence or reasoning].

**then** referral send rate will increase by 15%,
**because** users are most satisfied at the moment of first value realization.
