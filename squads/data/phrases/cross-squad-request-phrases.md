# Cross-Squad Request and Delivery Phrases

## Purpose

Standardized phrases for requesting data-related work from other squads and delivering data insights to them. Clear, respectful cross-squad communication reduces friction and speeds up collaboration.

## Making Requests to Other Squads

### Framing the Request
- "We have a data request that supports [business goal/initiative]. Here is what we need and why."
- "To answer [business question], we need [specific deliverable] from your team."
- "This request is [priority level] and ideally completed by [date]. Happy to discuss timeline if that does not work."
- "Context: [2-3 sentences explaining why this matters and what decision it enables]."

### Requesting from Engineering
- "We need event tracking added for [user action]. Here is the proposed event schema: [spec]."
- "The data pipeline for [source] has an issue: [description]. This is affecting [downstream impact]."
- "We need access to [table/system] for [purpose]. Estimated query pattern: [description]."
- "Could you review this query for performance? It currently runs in [X] minutes against [table], and we need it under [Y]."
- "We identified a data discrepancy that may originate in [system]. Can we schedule 15 minutes to investigate together?"

### Requesting from Product
- "To measure the impact of [feature], we need [instrumentation/definition/clarification]."
- "We need product input on the success criteria for [initiative] before we can design the measurement plan."
- "Can you confirm the intended behavior of [feature]? The data shows [unexpected pattern], and we want to verify whether this is by design."
- "We are planning the analysis for [initiative]. To do it well, we need [specific input] from your side by [date]."

### Requesting from Marketing
- "To improve attribution accuracy, we need consistent UTM parameters on [campaigns/channels]. Here is the proposed convention: [spec]."
- "Can you share the campaign calendar for [period]? We need it to contextualize traffic and conversion changes."
- "We need the final spend numbers for [campaign/period] to calculate ROI. Our deadline for the report is [date]."

### Requesting from CS
- "We need feedback on whether the health score alerts are actionable. Specifically: [questions]."
- "Can you share qualitative context for the [X] accounts that churned in [period]? Exit reasons in the CRM would help calibrate our model."
- "We are updating the account health model. Input on which signals CS finds most predictive would be valuable."

## Delivering to Other Squads

### Framing the Delivery
- "Here is the [analysis/report/data] you requested. Summary of findings below, full detail attached."
- "Your question was: [restated question]. The answer is: [direct answer]."
- "We completed the analysis on [topic]. Here are the key findings, translated for your context."

### Delivering to Engineering
- "The data confirms [technical finding]. Impact: [scope]. Recommended fix: [suggestion]."
- "Query performance analysis: [table] scans [X] rows per query. Suggested optimization: [approach]."
- "Pipeline health report: [X] jobs on time, [Y] delayed, [Z] failed. Details and prioritized fix list attached."

### Delivering to Product
- "Feature [name] adoption is at [X]% after [Y] weeks. Here is how it breaks down by segment."
- "The user behavior data shows [finding]. This [supports/challenges] the hypothesis behind [feature/initiative]."
- "Funnel analysis for [flow]: conversion rates at each step, drop-off points, and segment differences are in the attached."

### Delivering to Marketing
- "Campaign [name] performance: [X] conversions at $[Y] CAC. Attribution model: [method]. Full breakdown attached."
- "Channel efficiency report for [period]: [summary table]. Recommendations for budget reallocation included."
- "The landing page change [improved/reduced] conversion by [X]%. Recommendation: [action]."

### Delivering to CS
- "Updated health scores are live. [X] accounts moved to red, [Y] to yellow. Priority list and suggested actions attached."
- "Churn analysis for [period]: top risk factors, affected accounts, and recommended interventions included."
- "QBR data package for [account] is ready. Key talking points based on their usage data: [list]."

## Follow-Up Phrases

- "Did this answer your question? If you need a different cut or more detail, let us know."
- "We will check back in [X] days to see if this is being used and if adjustments are needed."
- "If the data raises new questions, we are happy to do a follow-up analysis."
- "Feedback on this delivery would help us improve future requests. What worked? What was missing?"
- "This is now documented at [link] for future reference."
