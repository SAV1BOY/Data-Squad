_Example:_

### Insight 1: Activation Improvements Are Translating to Revenue
The 6pp improvement in activation (28% to 34%) from the onboarding redesign is beginning to show in revenue. Cohorts activated in Oct-Nov show 12% higher M2 MRR than pre-redesign cohorts. If we maintain 34% activation, this projects to ~$18K additional MRR by end of Q1.

### Insight 2: Mid-Market Is the Churn Epicenter
72% of churned MRR came from mid-market accounts (50-500 employees). The top 3 churn reasons: missing integrations (38%), pricing (28%), and champion departure (22%). Integration gaps are addressable; pricing concerns warrant research.

### Insight 3: Referral Channel Quality Outperforms All Others
Referral customers show 52% higher 6-month retention and 38% higher average MRR than the channel blend. At $48 CPA vs $88 average, referral is both the cheapest and highest-quality channel. Current referral volume is only 10% of new customers.
