# North-Star Metric

This subdirectory contains deep-dive analyses, trend reports, and strategic context for the north-star metric.

## Purpose

The north-star metric (currently Monthly Active Users) is the single most important indicator of product health. This directory holds the most thorough and frequently updated analyses to ensure leadership always has a clear, contextualized view of this metric.

## What Goes Here

- Monthly and quarterly north-star metric reviews with trend analysis
- Driver decomposition: what factors are pushing the metric up or down
- Segment-level breakdowns (by plan, cohort, geography, acquisition channel)
- Forecasts and scenario analyses for the north-star metric
- Proposals for north-star metric changes or refinements
- Board-ready summaries and talking points

## Conventions

- Publish a monthly review by the 5th business day of each month
- Always include both the absolute number and the trend (month-over-month, year-over-year)
- Decompose any significant movement into contributing factors
- Reference the metric definition from the metric registry and DEC-001
- Keep a running log of anomalies and their explanations for historical context
